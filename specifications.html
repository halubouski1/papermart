Paper Weights

Five papers, from ultra-thin tracing to heavyweight. Pick the one that suits your work.

  • 35gsm Ultra Lightweight

    A white tracing paper, our thinnest. The same paper as 50gsm, only thinner.

    • Ideal for tracing
    • White, very thin
    • 24" & 36" widths

  • 50gsm Lightweight

    Our most popular paper for sewing patterns.

    • Smooth and rough side
    • Traces under certain lighting
    • 24" & 36" widths

  • 90gsm White

    A brilliant white paper with a smooth matt finish.

    • Brilliant white
    • Smooth matt finish
    • 24" & 36" widths

  • 90gsm Translucent

    Incredibly transparent for its density, ideal for tracing.

    • Does not crease easily
    • Easy to write on in pencil or pen
    • Strong and durable

  • 120gsm Heavyweight

    Our strongest paper, brilliant white with a smooth matt finish.

    • Extra strength and rigidity
    • Smooth matt finish
    • 24" width only

All papers are high quality and designed to print with sharp, accurate lines every time.
