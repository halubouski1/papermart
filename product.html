Product description

Our 5mm & 50mm grid paper with ruler is designed for precision and reliability. The printed ruler along the edge makes measuring and drawing straight lines easy and accurate.

The 50mm major grid lines are printed bolder so you can scale up drawings and patterns at a glance, while the fine 5mm grid keeps every measurement precise.

Supplied on a continuous roll, the paper lets you draft large pieces in one go without joins. It is suitable for pencil, pen and marker, and can be cut, folded and pinned without tearing.

  • 5mm fine grid for detailed work
  • 50mm bold grid for larger measurements
  • Ruler printed along one edge (0–100cm)
  • High quality print that won’t fade
  • Smooth paper for clean drawing and writing
