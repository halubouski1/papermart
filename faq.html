Part 1: Products, Specifications & Designs

Information about our grid designs, paper types, sizes and customisation options.

  • We offer metric grids (1mm, 5mm, 10mm, 20mm, 50mm and 100mm combinations, some with a printed ruler), imperial 1" & ½" grids, spot & cross papers in 1" and 20mm spacing, and plain pattern drafting paper.

  • Our grid papers are available in 50gsm and 90gsm, on white paper or translucent tracing paper. The options for each design are shown on its product page.

  • Rolls are available in 610mm (24") and 914mm (36") widths, in a range of lengths including a 20m value roll. Each product page lists the exact width and length options.

  • 50gsm is a lighter, thinner paper that is easy to trace through and handle in long lengths. 90gsm is heavier and more durable, ideal for drawings and patterns that are handled or reused often.

  • For pattern making we recommend our spot & cross or 1" & ½" grid papers. They make it easy to draft, grade and adjust patterns accurately.

  • Yes. Our precision metric grids, including the 1mm, 5mm & 10mm and the ruled designs, are well suited to technical drawing, engineering and design work.

  • Yes. We can print a grid or graph paper roll to your exact requirements, including layout, spacing, colour and measurements. Request a custom design and our team will help you get it right.

  • Our standard designs are printed in blue or red. For custom designs we can print in blue, red, black or other colours.

  • No. Every roll is printed at our production facility in Leicester, United Kingdom, and shipped directly to your door in Australia.
